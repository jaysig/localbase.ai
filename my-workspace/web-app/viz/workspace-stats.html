🔓 About Unhooking

LocalBase is designed for data mobility. Your data stays local, always. If you ever want to disconnect from the framework, you keep everything important: your databases (SQLite files), connectors (JavaScript), visualizations (HTML), and credentials (env.local). The framework provides the IDE and tools, but your data is never locked in. Export, migrate, or run standalone anytime.
